QUESTION 1:
Type: MCQ
Given the right epipole 𝑒′(1, 4) and a point 𝑥′(3, 2) in right image plane of a stereo imaging
set up, compute the epipolar line 𝑙′ containing the point 𝑥′, in right image plane in
homogeneous coordinate system.
a) (0.2, 0.2, 1)
b) (−0.2, 0.2, 1)
c) (−0.2, −0.2, 1)
d) (0.2, −0.2, 1)
Correct Answer: : c)
Detailed Solution: Epipolar line 𝑙′ = 𝑒′ × 𝑥′ in homogeneous coordinate system.
