QUESTION 6:
Type:MCQ
Which of the following is the primary purpose of histogram equalization?
a) Reduce image size
b) Improve image contrast
c) Remove image noise
d) Detect edges
Correct Answer: b)
Detailed Solution: Histogram equalization redistributes intensity values over the available
range to enhance contrast, especially in low-contrast images.

QUESTION 9:
Type: MCQ
Consider a binary image with a fully filled white square at the center on a black background.
How does this image look after applying image gradient operation?
a) Entire square remains white
b) A thin line surrounding the square boundary
c) Only the center pixel becomes white
d) Entire background becomes white
Correct Answer: b)
Detailed Solution: The gradient highlights intensity transitions only at object boundaries.
Since the square has constant intensity inside, only its edges are detected.
