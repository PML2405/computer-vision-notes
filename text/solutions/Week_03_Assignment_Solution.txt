QUESTION 10:
Type:MCQ
Consider a pinhole camera with camera center at the origin and image plane Z = f. A 3D
point X = (2, 4, 2) is projected onto the image plane with f = 1. Which statements are

(a) The image point is (2, 4).
(b) The projected point lies on the ray passing through the camera center and the 3D point.
(c) If the focal length is doubled, the image point also doubles in magnitude.
(d) The projection preserves straight lines in 3D not passing through the camera center as
straight lines in the image.
Correct options: (b), (c), (d)
