QUESTIONS 6:
Type: MSQ
Which of the following statements are true?
a) Image reconstruction using color demosaicing leads to sharp edges in an image.
b) Green channel is less likely to be aliased.
c) In CFA, green channels are sampled at a higher rate than red and blue.
d) High correlation between red, green and blue channels are very likely to have similar
texture and edge locations.
Correct Answer: b), c), d)
Detailed Solution:
Image reconstruction using color demosaicing lead to blurred edges in an image. In CFA,
green channels are sampled at a higher rate than red and blue. High correlation between red,
green and blue channels are very likely to have similar texture and edge locations. Green
channel is less likely to be aliased and details are preserved better in green channels
compared to red or blue.
